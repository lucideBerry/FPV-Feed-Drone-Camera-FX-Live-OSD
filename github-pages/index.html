Telemetry OSD

A real flight OSD, element by element.

The OSD is built from 21 procedural elements. Each one has its own position, scale, colour, outline, blink and shadow, and every number can be keyframed. Pick a layout to start from, then edit any element.

Flight bake

Animate the camera. The OSD flies with it.

One button keyframes every telemetry value from your camera animation, with quadcopter physics for throttle, current and voltage sag. The signal fades with distance and when terrain blocks the line of sight, and the look can follow it from clean to broken.

Built to behave

Safe in real projects.

FPV Feed sets itself up in one click and keeps your existing setup intact. When something needs your attention, Blender's status bar says what and how to fix it.

Getting Started checklist

Build, pick a source, a look, the intensity, a HUD layout, preview and render. Each step has its own button.

Keeps your compositor

A compositor the scene already had is kept, and one click puts it back together with your view settings.

Any source

Your scene render, a still, a numbered image sequence or a movie file. Footage of any shape is cropped to fill the frame.

Resolution independent

Sizes are set in pixels at 1080p or in percent of the height, so a look made at 720p looks the same at 4K.

Same frame, same image

Every effect is seeded and deterministic. Re-render a frame and you get exactly the same pixels.

Renders without the add-on

Everything is stored in the .blend, so files render on another machine or a farm without installing anything.
